How to Start an Insurance Business

Your 5-step roadmap to building a profitable insurance agency
1

Get Licensed

  • Complete pre-licensing coursework (40-60 hours)
  • Pass your state insurance exam
  • Apply for your license with the state DOI
  • Choose your lines: life, health, P&C
2

Build Your Foundation

  • Register your business (LLC recommended)
  • Get your E&O insurance
  • Contract with carriers (start with 3-5)
  • Set up your CRM and phone system
3

Launch & Grow

  • Build your online presence and website
  • Start with your warm market (100 contacts)
  • Create a referral system from day one
  • Target 10 policies in your first 30 days
