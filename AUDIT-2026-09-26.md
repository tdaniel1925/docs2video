# Docs2Video — full user-impact audit (2026-09-26)

Read from the code by five parallel reviewers (create flow, money, share page
and email, sign-in and security, everything else). Nothing was run against
production except a read-only list of production environment variable names.
Where a finding depends on what is actually in the production database, it
says so.

Severity: **Critical** = losing money, exposing data, or users stuck right now.
**High** = users charged for nothing, broken features, broken links.
**Medium** = confusing, wrong, or risky. **Low** = polish.

---

## Critical — fix first

| # | What goes wrong | Where |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Anyone signed in can give themselves free credits.** The database lets a user write their own `videos` rows with any `status`/`deducted_cost`; the stuck-video cron then "refunds" that amount as credits. Every new row is a fresh refund. | `supabase/migrations/20260707_videos_jobs_notifications_rls.sql:29-35`, `app/api/cron/fix-stuck-videos/route.ts:66-77` |
| C2 | **The card check can be skipped, and paying customers get downgraded.** `/api/confirm-card` marks a card on file and grants the trial without checking a card exists. A past-due or banned user can call it to lift their block; a Pro user who revisits `/setup-payment` is reset to "trial"; the `customerId` in the request is trusted, so a subscription can be created on someone else's saved card. | `app/api/confirm-card/route.ts:24-45` |
| C3 | **"Switch plan" bills subscribers twice.** It opens a second subscription instead of changing the first; the old one keeps charging and is no longer tracked. Cancelling the old one later drops the paying user to free. Trial users who upgrade also keep a 365-day trial subscription that charges their card after a year. | `app/(dashboard)/pricing/page.tsx:63`, `app/api/stripe/checkout/route.ts:25-133`, `app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts:228,366,439-442` |
| C4 | **Refused videos get stuck and users loop.** Generation marks the video "scripting" *before* checking card/credits. On a refusal the row stays stuck, every retry says "already being generated", it counts against the one-at-a-time limit, and every "not enough credits" sends the user to the card page and back again. | `app/api/generate-video/route.ts:285-383`, `app/(dashboard)/create/theme/page.tsx:132` |
| C5 | **Clients get chased after they've paid.** The daily follow-up job emails any quote still marked "sent" — and nothing ever marks a quote paid. No unsubscribe link, and the agent never turned it on. Depending on the production table, it either never runs, or sends several copies a day, out of order, every day. | `app/api/cron/follow-ups/route.ts:35-69,138,246-253`, `app/(dashboard)/videos/[id]/page.tsx:867` |
| C6 | **Logged-out visitors are sent to the login page** for the blog, contact page, campaign links (`/m/…`) and **unsubscribe links** (`/unsubscribe/…`). Google indexes redirects; email recipients can't unsubscribe (a CAN-SPAM problem). `/pricing` in the sitemap also bounces to login. | `proxy.ts:59`, `app/sitemap.ts`, `app/api/admin/campaigns/*` |
| C7 | **Someone can take over a user's email connection.** The Gmail/Outlook connect callbacks use the user id in `state` with no session check, so an attacker can attach their own mailbox to a victim's account; the victim's client emails then go out through the attacker. | `app/api/auth/google/callback/route.ts:8,45-58`, `app/api/auth/microsoft/callback/route.ts:8,46-58` |

## Medium

- **The price is computed from what the browser sends** (output type, length) — a user can pay the cheapest price for the full video. `generate-video/route.ts:359-365`
- **Brief/script go stale**: going Back and changing the document keeps the old brief and script; "Skip" doesn't skip the brief; the approved brief is ignored when writing the script; generated scripts are lost on reload and long ones time out. `brief/route.ts:37`, `brief/page.tsx:70-81`, `script/page.tsx:306-358`
- **Personal touches overridden**: the script page's own cover/closing replaces the presenter intro, client greeting and contact settings. `script/page.tsx:43-55`, `generate-video/route.ts:973-979`
- **Drafts are silently deleted after 24h**, finished scripts included. `app/api/cron/cleanup-drafts`
- **Raw server/Stripe errors shown to users** in generation, checkout, portal and credit packs.
- **Onboarding "Subscribe" fails for Starter and Agency** ("Invalid plan."); `/setup-payment` and the help center still sell the retired $29 Starter. `(onboarding)/setup/page.tsx:831`, `help/page.tsx:68,220,469`
- **Pricing page contradicts itself** (20 vs ~25 videos on Pro; "$5 per extra video" vs real overage rates). `app/_lib/pricing.ts:32,71`
- **Password reset / email confirm can dead-end**; after a reset, unfinished onboarding blocks the password form. `_actions/auth.ts:28,121-124`, `(dashboard)/layout.tsx:58`
- **Settings and onboarding say "Saved" even when saving failed**; a failed onboarding save loops back to setup. `settings/page.tsx:315-467`, `setup/page.tsx:307-324`
- **Open redirects** after card entry and in the MCP OAuth flow. `setup-payment/page.tsx:87`, `api/mcp/oauth/authorize/route.ts:16-43`
- **SMTP setup can probe internal servers; email passwords/tokens stored in plain text** and echoed back. `api/email-connections/route.ts:37-62`
- **Agents get a text + email on every view**, including their own previews; asking a question counts as a new view; no opt-out. `track-view/route.ts:9,322-402`
- **Converted clients flip back to "engaged"** on any rewatch; tracking stops once a video has two quotes. `track-view/route.ts:209-213,310`
- **"Ask a question" has no rate limit.** `watch/[id]/ask/route.ts`
- **Delete says it worked when it didn't**; quote edits revert paid quotes and always say "saved". `videos/[id]/page.tsx:867-905`
- **Leads can't unsubscribe** from nurture emails ("Invalid unsubscribe link"). `cron/nurture/route.ts:296`, `email-prefs/route.ts:24`
- **Nurture email promises "no card needed" and an automatic discount** — both untrue. `cron/nurture/route.ts:187,288-293`, `signup/page.tsx:18`
- **Commercial page "Buy more" goes to a 404** (`/billing`). `create/commercial/page.tsx:290`
- **Paying AI Social customers have no menu link to the tool.** `app/_lib/brand.ts:72-81`
- **Revoking a promo account leaves unlimited credits on**; promo emails send a guessable plaintext password. `admin/promo-user/route.ts:38,99-102`
- **Two migrations define conflicting subscription-status rules** — depending on production, bans silently fail or "change plan → agency" fails. `20260919_audit_missing_columns_and_rls.sql:90` vs `20260817…`
- **Apex sales by existing users aren't reported to Apex**; a failed account creation after payment is swallowed. `webhooks/stripe/route.ts:112,142,170`

## Low

Trial-to-paid can grant twice; partial refunds revoke a whole pack; promo codes work on credit packs; hardcoded live promo id; reconcile cron reads only 100 sessions; Gmail subjects with emoji arrive garbled; share emails always Docs2Video-branded; presentations count page loads as plays; analytics total may cap at 1,000; watch API returns a payment intent id; non-Calendly booking links get the small button; generating page stuck on unknown statuses; duplicate brands created on Back/Next; welcome email sent before email confirmation; admin pages load their shell for non-admins (data still blocked); no branded 404/error pages; thin page metadata; several optional env vars unset.
