## The Problem We Solve

Insurance agents spend significant time explaining complex illustrations to clients — documents that are 30-50 pages of projections, guarantees, riders, and legal language that most clients never fully read. Docs2Video transforms these illustrations into short, narrated video summaries that help clients understand their coverage.

The critical question is: **how do we do this while maintaining full legal compliance?**

## Our Multi-Layered Compliance Approach

We've implemented eight distinct layers of protection:

### 1. Carrier Name Redaction (Endorsement Protection)

Our system automatically detects and removes all insurance carrier names from both the video narration and visual slides. The narrator uses natural phrases like "this carrier" or "your carrier" instead of naming the company directly.

**Why this matters:** This prevents any appearance that the carrier endorses or authorized the video. Under NAIC advertising regulations, a carrier can be held responsible for third-party materials that appear to be endorsed by them. By removing the carrier name entirely, we eliminate this liability vector.

### 2. Automatic Disclaimer Extraction from Source Documents

When an agent uploads an illustration PDF, our AI extracts every disclaimer, disclosure, and legal notice from the original document — preserving the carrier's own compliance language verbatim. These extracted disclaimers are displayed on the video's viewing page, ensuring the carrier's required disclosures travel with the content.

**Why this matters:** NAIC Model Regulation #582 requires specific disclosure language to accompany illustrations. By extracting and preserving the carrier's own disclaimers, we ensure their carefully crafted compliance language remains intact and accessible to the viewer.

### 3. Opening Disclaimer Slide (In-Video)

Every insurance illustration video begins with a narrated disclaimer slide stating:

> "Before we begin, please note: this video is intended for educational and informational purposes only. It explains general concepts related to life insurance illustrations. It is not legal, tax, or financial advice. Policy guarantees are based on the claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company. Any non-guaranteed values shown are subject to change. Please review all policy materials and consult with your licensed insurance professional before making any decisions."

**Why this matters:** This disclaimer is part of the video file itself — it cannot be separated from the content regardless of how the video is shared or downloaded.

### 4. Closing Disclaimer Slide (In-Video)

Near the end of every insurance video, a closing disclaimer reinforces:

> "As a reminder, this video is for educational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Policy guarantees depend on the issuing carrier's claims-paying ability, and non-guaranteed values may change. Please review your official policy documents and consult with your licensed professional."

**Why this matters:** Bookending the video with disclaimers ensures the viewer is reminded both before and after consuming the content. This follows FINRA and SEC best practices for financial content.

### 5. Comprehensive Watch Page Disclosures

When a client views a video on our platform, the watch page displays a full "Legal Disclosures" section containing:

- **Carrier Disclosures:** The original disclaimers extracted from the illustration PDF
- **Platform Disclosures:** Our eight standard compliance statements covering:
  - Educational purpose only / not financial advice
  - Non-guaranteed values subject to change
  - Claims-paying ability dependency
  - Third-party non-endorsement statement
  - Official documents govern
  - Not a solicitation
  - Illustrated assumptions may not continue
  - Consult licensed professional

**Why this matters:** This ensures full compliance when the video is viewed on our platform, and provides a permanent, linkable record of all applicable disclosures.

### 6. Share Link Disclaimer Propagation

When an agent copies the share link to send to a client, our system automatically includes the full disclaimer text in the copied content — not just the URL. The agent pastes a pre-formatted message that includes:

- A professional introduction
- The video link
- The complete Important Disclosure statement

**Why this matters:** This ensures disclaimers travel with the content even when shared via email, text message, or any other channel outside our platform. The agent doesn't need to remember to add disclaimers — they're included automatically.

### 7. Non-Editable Disclaimer Protection

During the video creation process, agents can review and edit the narration script. However, the disclaimer slides are **locked and cannot be edited or removed.** The system clearly labels them as "Legal Disclaimer (required)" and prevents any modification.

**Why this matters:** This prevents agents from accidentally or intentionally removing required compliance language, protecting both the agent and our platform.

### 8. Script-Level Compliance Rules

Our AI narration system enforces several compliance rules at the script generation level:

- **No carrier names** in any narration text
- **No specific product recommendations** or comparisons
- **No guarantee language** beyond what the illustration itself states
- **Factual presentation** of data directly from the source document
- **Professional, educational tone** — not sales-oriented

## NAIC Model Regulation #582 Compliance

The NAIC Life Insurance Illustrations Model Regulation establishes requirements for illustration disclosures. Our framework addresses these requirements as follows:

| NAIC Requirement | Our Implementation |
|---|---|
| Non-guaranteed elements clearly identified | Narration explicitly distinguishes guaranteed vs. illustrated values |
| Disclosure that values may change | Both opening and closing disclaimer slides + watch page |
| No misleading representations | AI prevents exaggerated or misleading language; carrier name removed to prevent endorsement appearance |
| Illustration assumptions disclosed | Watch page includes assumption disclosure language |
| Policy contract governs | Explicitly stated in platform disclosures |

## Why This Protects You

1. **Agents are protected** because disclaimers are automatic and non-removable — they can't accidentally create non-compliant content.

2. **Agencies are protected** because every video has a documented compliance trail — disclaimer extraction, in-video disclaimers, and watch page disclosures create a clear record.

3. **Carriers are protected** because their names are never used (no endorsement implication), their original disclaimers are preserved, and the video explicitly states it's third-party content.

4. **Clients are protected** because they receive clear, understandable explanations of their coverage with all required disclosures — which is the fundamental purpose of NAIC Model #582.
