Every professional sends documents their clients don't read. Insurance illustrations sit in inboxes unopened. Financial reports get skimmed. Proposals lose deals because clients don't understand what they're looking at. You spent hours preparing it. They spent zero minutes reading it.

### The Solution
Docs2Video turns any document into a branded, narrated video explainer that your clients will actually watch. Upload your PDF, and in under two minutes you have a professional video that walks your client through every key detail — with your brand, your voice, your style.

### How It Works
1. **Upload or describe.** Drop any PDF, paste text, type an idea, or chat with our AI to build a proposal from scratch. Docs2Video reads any document type — financial reports, insurance illustrations, contracts, proposals, medical records, and more.

2. **AI does the work.** Our AI extracts every key data point, writes a clear narration script, generates branded presentation slides, and records a natural-sounding voiceover. You review the slides and approve before anything goes out.

3. **Share one link.** Your client gets a branded page with their explainer video, key data visualized, and options to book a meeting, ask questions via AI chatbot, or accept a quote and pay — all in one place. You get notified the moment they watch.
