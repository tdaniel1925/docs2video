## Recording Tips:
1. Use Loom in "Screen + Cam" mode (small face bubble in corner)
2. Record at 1920x1080
3. Speak naturally — not scripted-sounding
4. Move your mouse slowly and deliberately
5. Pause briefly on each important screen
6. Clean your browser (close other tabs, hide bookmarks bar)
7. Use a real document with real data (not lorem ipsum)
8. Total should be 90-120 seconds max
