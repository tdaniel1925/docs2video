# JORDYN — SCRIPT v2, for review

## What was wrong with v1

You were right on all of it. Naming it plainly so it does not come back:

- **It assumed you already knew who Jordyn was.** The word "Jordyn" appeared
  once, at second 29, with no explanation of what she IS. A viewer who lands
  cold has no idea whether this is software, a person, or an agency.
- **Dramatic instead of informative.** It was a mood piece. It never said
  what the product does, how it works, or what it costs.
- **No structure.** It went problem → feelings → vague promise. It never did
  problem → solution → what the solution does → how you get it.
- **No logo, no call to action.** It opened on an empty room and stopped.
  Nobody was told what to do next.
- **Imagery too arty.** Sad chairs and crying-woman energy. It needed to look
  like a professional commercial, not a perfume ad.

## The shape of v2

Four acts, in the order you asked for.

| | Act | Seconds | What it has to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Logo** | 0-3 | Establish the brand before anything else |
| 2 | **The problem** | 3-20 | Make the case that an owner needs this |
| 3 | **The solution — what she IS** | 20-32 | Say plainly what Jordyn is |
| 4 | **What she DOES** | 32-58 | Four concrete capabilities, shown |
| 5 | **How you get her + CTA** | 58-75 | Steps, trial, the ask |

## Notes on choices you should push back on if you disagree

**"Fifty thousand a year"** — I used a round US figure for a small-business
admin hire. If you want a different number, or none, say so. I would rather
cut it than have it be wrong.

**"Fifteen minutes"** — placeholder pending the setup facts. If real
onboarding takes longer, this changes. I will not ship a number I cannot
verify.

**The trust beat survived from v1.** It is the one part of the old script
that was doing real work — it answers the actual objection. But it now sits
AFTER the viewer knows what she is, which is where it belongs. In v1 it was
the whole film.

**Length is ~75s.** Longer than v1, because it now has to inform rather than
just feel. If that is too long for where you will run it, the cut is Act 4 —
four capabilities can become two.
